# Review Parse Rules (Template)

目标：抽象综述文章知识整合逻辑。

## 输入
- abstract/introduction/main sections/subsections/conclusion

## 输出字段
- organization_type: mechanism|disease|method|timeline|mixed
- paragraph_logic: claim/evidence/synthesis
- high_level_writing: knowledge_gap/future_directions/clinical_implications
- reasoning_focus: claim -> evidence quality -> synthesis confidence

## 约束
- 重点识别“整合方式”，避免逐句复述。
